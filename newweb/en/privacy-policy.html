Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — clearly and simply.

This Privacy Policy explains how Casual Brothers Ltd. collects, uses, and protects your personal data. We comply with the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Spanish LOPDGDD.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Contact Information: Name, email, phone number, and similar data when you fill out a form or contact us directly.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our services.
  • Respond to your inquiries or requests.
  • Send you updates or newsletters (with your consent).
  • Analyze usage of our website to enhance user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations under the UK DPA 2018, GDPR, and the Spanish LOPDGDD.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, UK DPA 2018, and the Spanish LOPDGDD, you have the following rights: Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction, Objection, and Data Portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@casualbrothers.com.

6. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Both jurisdictions offer an adequate level of data protection under applicable regulations.
